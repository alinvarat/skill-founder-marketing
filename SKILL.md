# Founder marketing

Use this for a campaign or channel plan. Use `product-marketing` when the deliverable is a reusable product positioning and voice reference. Reuse that reference if available.

1. Read the brief and relevant `founder/idea.md`, `competitors.md`, `numbers.json`, `pricing.md` and `panel/results.md`. Use real customer evidence first; a simulated panel is optional. Label missing evidence and assumptions.
2. Write positioning: buyer, problem, category, useful difference and nearest alternative. Give alternatives when helpful and recommend one tied to evidence.
3. Select 2–3 starting channels where the intended buyers spend attention. For each, give fit, cost/source or labelled estimate, action and success metric. Do not create a broad channel stack without a reason.
4. When a launch campaign is requested, make a dated 30-day calendar from launch −14 through +15 inclusive. Include message, format/channel and owner, objection-based hooks, approved opening offer/end date and useful recurring content. Use the requested narrower scope when applicable.
5. Set channel budgets and three weekly metrics/change thresholds. Compute an acquisition ceiling from contribution and an explicit conservative purchase horizon, minus relevant delivery/refund/retention costs. Simulated purchases do not establish lifetime value.
6. Write `founder/marketing.md`. Keep current approved prices, and never invent a discount. Use factual claims, disclose paid partnerships, and check applicable advertising rules when relevant. Panel quotes are research, never testimonials.

Done: positioning, dates, budget and metrics agree with the inputs. Distinguish actual customer evidence from simulation. A strategy request does not authorize posting, buyer outreach, spending or generating images.
